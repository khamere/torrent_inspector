Installing it

  1. Open Tampermonkey → Create a new script.
  2. Paste the contents of DarkPeers-Torrent-Inspector.user.js over what is there, and save.
  3. Reload the tracker. The badges appear on the listing; Inspect torrent sits at the bottom right of a torrent page.

Running the full DKOKTO Scene Edition as well? This one notices it on page load, stands down and logs a note to the console rather than doubling every badge. Run one or the other.

Reads, never writes

No requests, posts, uploads or downloads of any kind. No @grant, no @connect, no account or API access.

Green is not approval

A tick means the supported title checks passed — not that the media, the source history or the upload were verified.

Titles, not files

It checks the display title. Filenames, descriptions and source claims are still yours to check.

Nothing is sent

Notes, the trackers you tick and the collected audit live in your browser only, and no login or key is ever stored.

These screenshots come from offline demo pages with invented release names — no login, no account data, nothing fetched. The rendering is the script itself; only the rows are made up.
